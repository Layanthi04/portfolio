Who I Am

About Me

A little about me and what drives me.

I’m a motivated and detail-oriented BICT undergraduate with a strong interest in Quality Assurance and a growing passion for UI/UX design. I have a solid understanding of SDLC, STLC, and Agile/Scrum methodologies, along with experience in manual testing and technical documentation.

I enjoy analyzing systems, identifying potential issues, and paying attention to the small details that contribute to reliable and user-friendly software. Alongside QA, I’m also interested in UI/UX design and creating interfaces that are both visually appealing and easy to use.

I’m always eager to learn new technologies, improve my skills, collaborate with others, and gain hands-on experience through real-world projects. My goal is to build a career in Quality Assurance while continuing to develop my skills and creativity in UI/UX design.
